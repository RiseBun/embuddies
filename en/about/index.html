embuddies connects hardware projects, their creators and the resources needed to make them.

What we do

We welcome every kind of hardware project, whether it is an open build or a showcase. Clear labels tell you what can be made from the available resources.

Creators keep their code, CAD and models on their preferred platforms. We are also planning a hardware hackathon and a creator community. Our embodied-agent and infrastructure work remains in development.
